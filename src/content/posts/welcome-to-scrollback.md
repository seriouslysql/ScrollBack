Scrollback is a blog theme built on [AstroPaper](https://github.com/satnaing/astro-paper).
It keeps everything AstroPaper does well: fast pages, light and dark mode, search and RSS.
It adds a topic browser on the home page, category pages built from your posts, post type
badges, Giscus comments support, and optional Decap CMS so you can write posts from a browser.

Delete these demo posts when you're ready. [Writing your first post](/posts/writing-your-first-post/)
covers the frontmatter and how publishing works.
